# Landing Copy

## Hero

**Headline:** Audit and optimize your AI tool spend in minutes.

**Subheadline:** Enter your tools, plans, seats, and monthly spend. AuditAI flags overkill plans, redundant subscriptions, and credit-based savings opportunities—then estimates what you can save.

**CTA button:** Start the audit

## Feature Copy

What you’ll get:

- **Per-tool actions**: concrete recommendations with numeric savings and a clear reason.
- **Savings math**: monthly and yearly totals, plus cost-per-seat where applicable.
- **Shareable report**: a public URL you can send to cofounders or finance after the report is persisted.

## How it works (feature bullets)

1. **Input your stack**
  - Tools, plan types, monthly spend, seats, team size, and use case.
2. **Rules run**
  - Deterministic checks for plan optimization, redundancy, and credit/usage mismatches.
3. **See savings**
  - Prioritized recommendations with numeric savings totals.

## FAQ

**Do I need an account to run an audit?**  
No. AuditAI is designed to work without login. You only submit an email optionally after you reach results.

**Are the savings numbers computed by AI?**  
No. The savings totals and recommendations are generated by deterministic rules in the audit engine. The LLM is used only to generate a short executive summary narrative.

**How do shareable report links work?**  
After you generate results, the app persists your deterministic report and provides a share URL under `/report/[id]`.

**Do you store my email?**  
If you opt in on the results page, your email (and optional company/role/team size) is stored securely for optional follow-up communication.

**What if I enter a tool that isn’t in the pricing catalog?**  
You can use **Other** to name the tool, but some pricing-based rules may not produce numeric target spends for unknown tools. The engine will still remain deterministic and may produce `NO_CHANGE` when no modeled pricing-based opportunity is detected.

## Trust-building Copy

- **Deterministic savings first:** numeric recommendations are rule-based and stable for the same inputs.
- **Clear assumptions:** savings math relies on explicit pricing assumptions stored in the codebase.
- **AI is optional and guarded:** the executive summary is best-effort and falls back automatically when needed.
- **Share internally, align fast:** a public report URL lets you gather feedback from decision-makers without re-running analysis.
